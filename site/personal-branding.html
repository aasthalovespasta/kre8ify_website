Questions

Questions about branding.

Do I have to write anything?

No. You talk, we write. You read and approve each post, and you can change anything before it goes live.

How much of my time does it take?

About 90 minutes a month for the recorded conversation, plus a few minutes a week to approve posts.

What if I do not like a post?

Tell us what feels off and we rewrite it. Nothing is published without your approval.

Is it only LinkedIn?

LinkedIn is where most B2B buyers are, so we start there. Posts can be adapted for Instagram or X if it helps.
