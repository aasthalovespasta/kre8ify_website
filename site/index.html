FAQ

Questions founders ask.

Which service should I start with?

It depends on where you are. Most founders start with personal branding or social media, then add GTM engineering once there is attention to convert. On the call we will tell you what makes sense.

I haven't registered my company yet. Is it too early?

No. Our 0 to 1 brand building service starts there: company set-up with partner CAs, your name and identity, and a clear offer.

What is GTM engineering, in plain words?

It is the system that finds the people most likely to buy from you and starts conversations with them: lists, outreach, quick replies and a CRM, running every week.

Will the content really sound like me?

Yes. We start with a recorded conversation, write in your voice, and nothing goes live without your approval.

Do you shoot content and work with creators?

Yes. Our social media and influencer marketing services cover shoots, reels, creator briefs and on-ground events.

How is pricing decided?

It depends on what you need, so Krish walks you through it on the call once he understands your business.
