Questions

What founders ask first.

I haven't registered my company yet. Is it too early?

No. That is where we start: structure, registration and GST in the first two weeks, with partner CAs handling the filings.

What is GTM engineering, in plain words?

It is the system that finds the people most likely to buy from you and starts conversations with them. We set up the lists, the outreach, the replies and the CRM, so it runs every week without you chasing it.

When will I see results?

Your profile and first posts are live within three weeks. Outreach starts around week five, and first meetings usually land between weeks five and eight.

Will the posts really sound like me?

Yes. We start with a 90-minute recorded conversation, and nothing goes live without your approval.

Can I start with just one of the three?

Yes. Each one works on its own. On the call we will tell you which one will help most right now.

How is pricing decided?

It depends on what you need, so Krish walks you through it on the call once he understands your business.

What don't you do?

Paid ads, SEO and website builds. If you need those, we will point you to someone good.
