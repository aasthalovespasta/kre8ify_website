Who it is for

For founders at the very start.

  • You have an idea and no company yet

    You want to start properly, without guessing which form comes first.

  • You registered, but have no clients

    The company exists on paper. Now it needs a brand, an offer and buyers.

  • You have a few clients and no system

    Work comes from luck and referrals. You want something you can repeat.

How it works

Five steps, in the right order.

    Set up the company

    Structure, registration, PAN, GST and a founders' agreement with our partner CAs.

    Weeks 1 to 2
  2. 02

    Weeks 1 to 3
  3. 03

    Shape your offer

    Weeks 2 to 3
  4. 04
