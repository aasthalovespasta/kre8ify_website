10Questions
What founders ask first.

    

      
I haven't registered my company yet. Is it too early?
No, that's exactly where stage 1 starts: structure, registrations and GST in weeks 1 and 2, with partner CAs handling the filings.

      
When will I see results?
Your profile and first posts are live within three weeks. Outreach goes live around week 5, and first meetings usually land in weeks 5 to 8.

      
Will the posts really sound like me?
Yes. We start with a 90-minute voice capture interview, and nothing is published without your approval.

      
Can I start with just one engine?
Yes. Each engine works on its own. On the call we tell you which one pays back fastest for your business.

      
What does it cost?
Fixed scope, timeline and fee, sent within 48 hours of the discovery call. No tool mark-ups.

      
What don't you do?
Paid ads, SEO and website builds. Depth in the 0→1 system is the point; for anything else we refer you to someone good.
